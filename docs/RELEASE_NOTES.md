Rovidx Teleprompter turns an ultrawide monitor into a reading studio, with adjustable light panels on either side of your script.

- Resizable three-panel layout with independent or linked studio lights.
- Plain-text file loading, paste, and recent scripts.
- Manual scrolling, steady auto-scroll, and offline English voice following.
- Adjustable typography, colors, reading guide, and camera position.
- Fullscreen mode, monitor selection, and saved preferences.
- Companion Codex skill for preparing scripts without changing their wording.

**Downloads:** Windows x64 installer or portable app; macOS Apple Silicon and Intel disk images or ZIP archives; Linux x64 and ARM64 AppImages or Debian packages. Choose the file matching your operating system and processor. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

Windows and Linux builds are unsigned. macOS builds are ad-hoc signed and are not notarized. Your operating system may ask you to approve the app. Offline voice mode downloads its verified English model once (about 70 MB), then processes microphone audio locally without an account, recording, or upload.

Built by [Rovidx](https://rovidx.com). See the [README](https://github.com/rovidxmedia/rovidx-teleprompter#readme) for setup and controls, or [report an issue](https://github.com/rovidxmedia/rovidx-teleprompter/issues).
